1. WEBSITE AND PROJECT OVERVIEW

MediYatra is presented as a Nepal-based healthcare coordination and medical
travel support service. The website explains support for patients travelling
from Nepal to a chosen hospital in India, and nursing care requested for home
visits. It distinguishes coordination support from clinical care: patients
choose their providers, and diagnosis, treatment and clinical decisions remain
with qualified doctors and hospitals.

The project is a pnpm/Turborepo monorepo containing:
- apps/web: Next.js 14 website, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and next-intl.
- apps/api: NestJS API with Prisma and PostgreSQL, Redis/BullMQ, and integration
  adapters for external messaging and storage services.
- packages/shared: shared TypeScript contracts and Zod schemas.

The web application includes pages/routes for the homepage, About, team,
success stories, blog, clinic, contact, medical tourism, nursing care,
treatments, hospitals, doctors, destinations, packages, consultation, quote,
login, registration, patient dashboard, admin, FAQ, legal information and
travel assistance, among others.

2. HOMEPAGE CONTENT AND PATIENT JOURNEY

The homepage currently includes:
- A hero with MediYatra positioning, Nepal-to-India journey messaging, and
  calls to plan a medical journey or book nursing care.
- A rotating medical-image background and a journey badge showing the
  Nepal-to-India-to-home flow.
- A trust strip covering case coordination, guided support, travel planning
  and personal support.
- About MediYatra and its role in practical coordination.
- Medical tourism coordination services, including report organization,
  hospital and doctor appointment coordination, documentation guidance,
  travel, local transport, accommodation, family support and follow-up.
- A seven-step patient journey, from sharing a requirement through return-home
  and follow-up coordination.
- Specialty categories such as oncology, cardiology, neurology, orthopaedics,
  gastroenterology, nephrology, urology, pulmonology, women's health,
  pediatrics and multispecialty care.
- A clear notice that qualified doctors and hospitals make clinical decisions.
- A doctor carousel when doctor profiles are available.
- Reasons to choose MediYatra and location information about Nepal and India.
- Nursing-care-at-home service descriptions.
- Patient feedback cards, a Google reviews panel, FAQs, contact details and
  calls to action.

5. PLATFORM CAPABILITIES DOCUMENTED IN THE PROJECT

The repository README describes these existing application/API capabilities:
- Public catalog reads and patient inquiry submission.
- Private medical-report uploads using short-lived upload links.
- Patient profile and dashboard requests.
- Appointment requests; requests remain pending until confirmed.
- Moderated reviews and contact-message handling.
- An admin inquiry pipeline.
- Authentication flows using Argon2id and access/refresh JWTs.
- Role, CSRF and request-throttling protections.
- Encryption for inquiry, contact and review text.
- Prisma persistence, PostgreSQL, Redis and BullMQ.
- S3-compatible signed document URLs and optional Resend/Twilio adapters.
- Same-origin web BFF routes that forward to the API and preserve session
  cookies.

The README also identifies areas needing further product implementation or
verification, including remaining domain CRUD/CMS, payment webhooks,
automated data-request fulfillment, provider-owned admin access and two-factor
authentication. Provider listings, prices, legal text, contact channels and
service availability must be verified before publication. Legal content needs
qualified review. Email and SMS delivery depend on configured provider
credentials.
